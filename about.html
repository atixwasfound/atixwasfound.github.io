About Me

The story so far.

Hi, I'm Atix.

I'm Atiksh Sharma, a 9th grader from India who loves building and tinkering with software and hardware. I am deeply passionate about systems programming, low-level engineering, and exploring how things work under the hood.

I like building compilers, experimental operating systems (like RadianOS), and tools that make programming more fun. I believe in learning by building, whether it's causality engines for satellites or offline AI rovers. Also, I really like cats.
